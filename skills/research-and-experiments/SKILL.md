



data structure:
experiments - folder:
  - readme.md - every experiment, with short description.
  - <topic> - folder:
    - index.md - detailed description of the experiment, index of the experiment, logs, wiki llm connections, etc.
    - <experiment_name>.md - detailed description of the experiment, logs, wiki llm connections, etc. maybe it connect a folder with the same name, with more details, logs, etc.


Important steps when creating an experiment:
1. what we try to achieve with the experiment, what is the goal, what is the expected outcome.
2. read the related experiments, research papers, etc. to understand the current state of the art and what has been done before.
3. think about hypothesis, what do we expect to happen, what are the possible outcomes, etc.
4. make research about the hypothesis do one check it? what you need to learn about the topic to be able to check the hypothesis? what are the possible ways to check the hypothesis? what are the pros and cons of each way?
5. design the experiment, what are the steps, what are the variables, what are the controls, what are the measurements, etc.
6. run the experiment, collect data, analyze the results, etc.
7. draw conclusions, what did we learn from the experiment, what are the implications, what are the next steps, etc. - you need to collect the important insights, learnings, etc. from the experiment and write them down in a clear and concise way, so that others can understand and learn from it. when you write the conclusions think about what we need to do next.
